# Register View options design (EXF-529, EXF-724, EXF-722)

**EXF-529:** the register toolbar's `View` button and its "View Options" popover (six presets, From/To month and year selects, Show Reconciled Transactions, Show Running Balance, Show Flags, Show Images, Cancel, Apply), kept per account and once for All Accounts; the BALANCE column, hidden reconciled rows with the footer and `View All`, the date range, the flag column; REST `GET`/`PUT …/accounts/{account_id}/view_settings` and `…/view_settings/all_accounts` with four MCP tools; `include=running_balance` on the account's transaction list.
**EXF-724:** in reconcile mode, a click on a row's cleared icon moves the header's Cleared/Uncleared and the bar's sentence and button at once; the bar's button is disabled while the figures are not confirmed.
**EXF-722:** a browser test that the Reconcile popover stays closed when Escape (or a click outside) closes it while the Yes request runs.

## The source app in this Build

The source app is the owner's real account. The only plan anyone may open is the test plan, at `https://app.ynab.com/542b334b-696e-4418-937a-5e2c21a8b45c/budget`. Its accounts are closed accounts that hold test rows only.

**What may be looked at and done:** the page of one account of the test plan (reached from the test plan's sidebar) and the All Accounts page of the test plan. On them: the toolbar's View button and its popover (the presets, the From and To selects, the four checkboxes, Cancel and Apply) may be opened, measured and captured. **In that popover, and only there, a setting may be changed and applied to see what the register then shows (the BALANCE column, hidden reconciled rows and the footer, a date range, the flag column), on one account and on All Accounts. Before the first change, write down the account's settings as found; when done, set them back and apply, and say so in the report with the before and after.** Reason: view settings are the test plan's own display state; the owner gave the test plan for any purpose; nothing else is changed by them.

**What nobody ever does in the source, each with its reason:**
- **Reconcile: never open it, never press anything in it.** Reason: a Designer pressed Next in the source's Reconcile and the whole session signed out 31 seconds later; every Build stopped until the owner signed in again. The reconcile parts of this Build (EXF-724, EXF-722) are compared with nothing in the source.
- **Create, edit, delete, clear, flag, approve or categorize a transaction; add, edit, reopen or delete an account; type into a register row.** Reason: it is the owner's account; one Designer who made accounts "to have something to compare" went through screens the owner has not released.
- **Open the plan menu, Bank Connections, Reflect, settings, the account's edit dialog, or any other plan.** Reason: they show the owner's data or are not released; nothing of this Build is there.
- **Leave the tab changed:** a size or theme override is set back, the view settings are as found, and the tab ends on the test plan's Plan page. Reason: two earlier Designers left the tab at a wrong size, and the next step's check then failed.

If the test plan's accounts cannot show a state (for example no reconciled row, so no footer), that state is "not compared: built from the ticket", which is a complete and acceptable answer.

**Each step states in its report, for itself only, every action it took in the source app (clicks, applied settings and overrides included), or that it opened nothing.**

## What exists (read in the code)

- `RegisterToolbar.tsx`: an inert View button in `.toolbar-group.right` (only Record Payment, Undo, Redo overflow into More).
- `RegisterTable.tsx` holds the list and sorts with `sortTransactions` (ties: amount ascending, `created_at`, `id`); `EntryRow`, `ScheduledSection`, `SplitLines` draw their own flag cells and `colSpan`s.
- `listAccountTransactions`: **5 D1 calls** (plan, account, rows, balances, lines).
- `ui_state (budget_id, key, value, updated_at)`, key `(budget_id, key)`; `getUiState` ignores keys outside `UI_STATE_FIELDS`.
- `useSetCleared` updates only the row optimistically; header and bar read `["accounts", budgetId, accountId]`, which moves after the PATCH **and** a refetch (the EXF-724 lag).
- `ReconcileContext.finish` moves Yes to "done" only if the step is still "ask" (the EXF-722 guard).

## Decisions

- **D1 Storage: the existing `ui_state` table, no migration.** Keys `view_settings:<account_id>` and `view_settings:all_accounts`, value = the settings JSON. Reason: keyed per plan with free-form keys and a primary-key read; a table would add a migration for six fields. `getUiState` keeps ignoring these keys. Unlike `ui-state`, writes log to `change_log`: `entity_type "view_settings"`, `entity_id` the account id or `all_accounts`, `create`/`update`, before/after settings, the actor. The reserved migration name is not used. A stored value failing the schema reads as the defaults.
- **D2 Settings shape.** `preset: this_month | last_month | latest_3_months | this_year | last_year | all_dates | custom`, `range_from`, `range_to` (months `YYYY-MM-01`, both set only for `custom`, else null), `show_reconciled` (default false), `show_running_balance` (false; account only), `show_flags` (true), `show_images` (true). Defaults when nothing is stored: `all_dates`. A preset is stored by name and resolved against the local today on each render. PUT merges a partial body: a non-custom `preset` clears the range; `range_from`/`range_to` without `preset` mean `custom`; `custom` must end with both months set (422 otherwise); `preset` other than custom together with a range is 422. From later than To is accepted (no rows, the footer), as in the source.
- **D3 Operations.** Answer `{ data: { view_settings } }`.
  - `GET|PUT /budgets/{budget_id}/accounts/{account_id}/view_settings` → `AccountViewSettings` (`account_id` + all fields). A closed account works (as `GET …/transactions`); a deleted or another plan's account → 404 `Account`; unknown plan → 404 `Plan`.
  - `GET|PUT /budgets/{budget_id}/view_settings/all_accounts` → `AllAccountsViewSettings` (no `account_id`, no `show_running_balance`; a PUT with it is 422, strict body).
  - MCP: `get_account_view_settings`, `update_account_view_settings`, `get_all_accounts_view_settings`, `update_all_accounts_view_settings`; coverage file `apps/worker/test/mcp-coverage/view-settings.ts`; four `CALLS` lines in `mcp-timestamps.test.ts` (results hold no timestamp).
- **D4 D1 calls.** GET account settings: plan (1) + account (1) + setting row (1) = **3** (two beyond the plan check). GET All Accounts: plan + row = **2**. PUT account: plan + account + before row + one batch `[upsert, change_log]` = **4**. PUT All Accounts: **3**. The account list with `include=running_balance`: **5, the same as without it**, for 10 and for 1,000 rows (the balance is a window-function subquery joined into the rows query). A counting test asserts all six numbers and 10 = 1,000.
- **D5 Running balance in SQL.** `include=running_balance` on `GET …/accounts/{account_id}/transactions` (and `include: "running_balance"` on the `list_transactions` tool with `account_id`; refused without it) adds `running_balance` (milliunits) to each row. Subquery over **every** live, non-line row of the account: `sum(amount) over (order by date asc, amount desc, created_at desc, id desc rows unbounded preceding)` — the reverse of the register's default order; each balance counts every earlier row, also rows that `since_date`, `needs`, the date range or the reconciled filter leave out. Fixed per row whatever the sort. Scheduled rows have none. The plan-wide list takes no `include`.
- **D6 The page cuts the rows, from one request.** The register already loads the whole list; the account register always asks `include=running_balance` (same request and key). The pure function `registerVisible(rows, settings, today, reconcileMode)` (schema package) returns the rows to show and `hidden` (count). Apply changes only the settings query: no refetch of rows. Settings come from `["view-settings", {budgetId, scope}]` — **an object as second key element**, so the plan-wide `queryKey.includes(budgetId)` invalidations never refetch it (no extra request per cleared click). The table shows "Loading transactions…" until both queries are there; a failed settings read uses the defaults.
- **D7 What is hidden.** Rows whose `cleared` is `reconciled` unless `show_reconciled`; rows whose month is outside [from, to] (inclusive, by month) unless `all_dates`. Scheduled rows and the entry row are never hidden. **In reconcile mode the date range is not applied** (an uncleared row outside it must be clearable); reconciled rows stay hidden (nothing to clear on them). **A row that becomes reconciled while the page is open hides as soon as the page holds it as reconciled** (after Finish's refetch), not at the next load or Apply as the Lead expected: the ticket's AC2 says the rows "disappear" after reconciling, and a rule on the held data alone keeps no hidden state.
- **D8 Footer.** When `hidden > 0`: after the last row (inside the table, a full-width row) the text "Some transactions are hidden by filters." and a secondary `View All` button. `View All` → PUT `{ preset: "all_dates", show_reconciled: true }` (the source's behaviour). With every row hidden, the footer replaces "No transactions".
- **D9 Columns.** A `RegisterViewContext` (`showFlags`, `showBalance`) from `Register` is read by `RegisterTable`, `EntryRow`, `ScheduledSection`, `SplitLines`: no flag `<col>`/cells when `showFlags` is false; `col-balance` (10%) and `cell-balance num` cells after INFLOW when `showBalance` (account page only). Header "BALANCE", not sortable; body `screenMoney(running_balance)`, `is-negative` → `var(--labelNegative)`; entry, scheduled and split-line rows: empty cell. `colSpan`s follow. **Show Images is stored and changes nothing** (no image column until EXF-536).
- **D10 Popover.** `View` opens `Popover` (`placement="below"`, `width={632}`, new `role="dialog"`, label "View Options"; it already clamps inside the window). A draft copied on open; presets save at once and close; selects set `custom` (no pill); Apply saves and closes; Cancel, Escape, outside click, View again discard. Years: from `min(first year, this year − 1)` to `this year + 10`, where first month = the earlier of the plan's `created_at` month (`useCurrentPlan`) and the oldest held row's month; All Dates shows From first month, To this month + 10 years. The tooltip is the app's `Tooltip` (above), not a native `title`, so it can be captured. Saves are optimistic (rolled back on error).
- **D11 1024×700.** View stays **inline in the toolbar** (the right group never overflows; as the source). The popover lies inside the window. With BALANCE on, amount cells (outflow, inflow, balance) are never cut for amounts up to −$99,999.99 (`col-amount`/`col-balance` keep a minimum width, the text columns give way); payee, category and memo cut with an ellipsis and keep their `title`.
- **D12 EXF-724 fix.** `useSetCleared` (`mutationKey ["set-cleared", budgetId]`) in `onMutate` also cancels and updates `["accounts", budgetId, row.account_id]` (`cleared_balance ± amount`, `uncleared_balance ∓ amount`, `balance` unchanged) and the list's `balances`; `onError` restores them. Header and bar move in the icon's render. `ReconcileBar`'s button is disabled while a set-cleared mutation of the plan is pending or the account query is fetching (`useIsMutating`, `useIsFetching`). No new request: one PATCH per click, invalidations as before (not the view-settings query, D6). **Measure first** (Implementer, local `pnpm dev`, scratch Playwright script, 10 and 500 rows, clear and unclear): click → icon, → header Cleared, → bar sentence, before and after. Live is not measured (nobody calls it).

### Looking

For each visual acceptance case, at 1440×900 and at 1024×700: the Tester drives the clone to that state, takes a **fresh** screenshot in this Thread (not a file a spec or an earlier step wrote), opens it, and writes one sentence on what is seen in it. The report lists `case, size, file, sentence` for every one. A case without its two entries is PARTIAL with the words "not looked at". "A spec is green" is not looking. The states to look at, at both sizes: the toolbar with the View button and its tooltip; the popover as it opens (All Dates highlighted); a preset chosen and the From and To selects following it; the register with the BALANCE column (a negative balance in red); reconciled rows hidden with the footer "Some transactions are hidden by filters." and `View All`; after View All (the rows with their locks); a date range that hides rows (the header's balances unchanged); the flag column hidden; the All Accounts popover (no Running Balance option); reconcile mode's bar right after a click on a cleared icon (EXF-724).
