- P2 [applied] `--grid-min` and `GRID_MIN` are 428px (were 440): the 444 px grid box has a 15 px vertical scrollbar (clientWidth 429), so 440 made it scroll 11 px sideways. The 12 px come from the narrow Assigned minimum (`.cell-assigned` 6.5rem → 5.75rem = 92 px, "$1,200.00" needs about 62 px); names get 58 px. Available keeps 7.5rem. Not comparable with the source (tab never resized).
